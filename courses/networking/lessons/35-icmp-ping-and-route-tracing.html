Understand the idea

Learning target: Use network control messages as evidence and understand the limits of ping and traceroute results.

ICMP carries network control information

ICMP is not merely ping. It reports conditions such as destination problems, time exceeded, and packet-size constraints. Echo request and reply are the familiar basis of ping. ICMPv6 additionally supports important IPv6 functions, including neighbour discovery. The permitted message types matter more than a blanket label saying ICMP is on or off.

A ping result tests a particular exchange

A successful echo exchange shows that those echo packets and replies worked at that time. It does not prove a web port, certificate, or database login works. A missing echo reply can reflect filtering or rate limits rather than a dead host. Test the actual service protocol when that is the reported problem.

Route tracing samples responses along a path

Traceroute-style tools use limited TTL or Hop Limit values and inspect replies. Missing intermediate replies do not prove transit traffic stopped at that hop. Routers may forward normally while declining or rate-limiting diagnostic replies. Load balancing can also make successive probes show different paths.

Work through one example

A traceroute prints an asterisk at one hop and later shows the destination.

  1. The intermediate router did not return the expected diagnostic response within the timeout.
  2. Later responses show that some probes travelled beyond that point.
  3. Treat the asterisk as missing diagnostic evidence, not automatic proof that forwarding stopped.

Practical troubleshooting

Symptom

Ping fails but the required HTTPS request works.

Evidence that narrows the cause

The echo path or policy differs from the actual service exchange.

Next action and verification

Report service success and separately document diagnostic ICMP handling.

Retest the original operation from the same source context. A successful test of a different endpoint or protocol does not establish that this symptom is resolved.
